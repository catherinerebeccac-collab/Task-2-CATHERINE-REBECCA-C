2. Since the bat costs ₹100 more than the ball, we can represent its cost as (x + 100).
3. The total cost of the bat and the ball together is given as ₹110. So, we can set up the equation:
   x + (x + 100) = 110
4. Now, let's solve for x by combining like terms:
   2x + 100 = 110
5. Subtracting 100 from both sides gives us:
   2x = 10
